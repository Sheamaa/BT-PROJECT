About the program

What is hospital volunteering?

The HPAP Hospital Volunteering Program gives students the opportunity to contribute to healthcare services at Hamad Medical Corporation. Volunteers support medical staff, assist patients, and gain firsthand exposure to the healthcare environment. The program is designed to develop empathy, communication skills, and professional awareness in students who are interested in medicine and healthcare careers.

Requirements

Eligibility criteria

The program is open to both high school and undergraduate students who meet the following requirements:

High school students

  • 15 or above years old
  • Valid QID
  • Available in the morning
  • Available on weekdays

Undergraduate students

  • 1st year or above
  • Enrolled in a institution
  • Valid QID
  • Available in the morning
  • Available on weekdays

Important notes

  • All volunteering activities take place from Sunday to Thursday only. Friday and Saturday slots are not available.
  • Each student may hold only one active hospital volunteering placement at a time.
  • Slots are limited per department per week and allocated on a first-come first-served basis upon staff approval.
  • Undergraduate and graduate students may apply for high school departments.
  • Undergraduate students may also apply for undergraduate-level departments.
  • High school students may only apply for high school departments.
