Hospital Volunteering

Join our community of dedicated volunteers making a difference in healthcare. Gain valuable experience while helping patients and staff across Hamad Medical Corporation departments in Qatar.

Register Now

Who Can Apply?

The program is open to high school and undergraduate students who meet the following criteria

High School Students

  • 15 or above years old
  • Valid QID
  • Available in the monring
  • Available on weekdays

Undergraduate Students

  • 1st year or above
  • Enrolled in a institution
  • Valid QID
  • Available in the monring
  • Available on weekdays

Parents

  • Can apply on behalf of their child
  • Child must meet eligibility criteria
  • Valid QID for the child

How It Works

Four simple steps to start your volunteering journey

1

Create Account

Sign up and complete your profile with your personal and academic information

2

Get Recommendation

Answer a short questionnaire and let our smart algorithm suggest the best department for you

3

Submit Application

Apply to your chosen department, select your preferred week, and upload required documents

4

Start Volunteering

Receive approval, complete your volunteering activity, and earn your certificate

Important Notes

  • All volunteering activities take place Sunday to Thursday only
  • Each student may hold only one active hospital volunteering placement at a time
  • Slots are limited per department per week and allocated on first-come first-served basis
  • Undergraduate and graduate students may apply for high school departments
  • High school students may only apply for high school level departments
  • Students can register for multiple workshops simultaneously

This platform helps students and parents apply and get matched to suitable volunteering departments efficiently. Our smart recommendation system guides you to the best placement based on your interests and availability.
